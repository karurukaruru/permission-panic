# Allow-key campaign

The key image was made with the built-in image-generation tool, with
`transparent_background: true`, then copied to `permission-key.png`.
The typography and poster layouts are deterministic HTML/CSS in `launch.html`.
No other company's logo, endorsement, product UI or campaign slogan is used.
The image is a metaphor for an approval decision, not a real hardware product.

## Final image prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / product-mockup. Create a polished premium campaign key
visual as ONE sculptural physical keyboard-style permission button, isolated with
genuine transparent background. It is an editorial metaphor for an AI coding
agent permission game called Permission Panic, not a screenshot or a real product.
One generously wide rectangular keycap with beautifully rounded but restrained
corners, warm ivory satin ceramic top, a fine frosted glass perimeter and pale
brushed-aluminum lower lip. Perspective: elevated three-quarter front view, top
face clearly legible; the object floats in space, gentle tilt of about 12 degrees,
like meticulous high-end industrial design studio product photography. The only
text on the object is 'Allow' in elegant small dark graphite sans-serif letters,
exact spelling, professionally centered. There is a tiny warm coral-red sliver of
light just under the lower right seam, suggesting a hidden consequence,
restrained and sophisticated, not sci-fi. Materials must feel tactile and
expensive: broad softbox highlights, beautifully smooth surface, subtle realistic
microtexture. Gentle physically plausible translucent shadow immediately beneath
the floating object, kept within alpha. Full object visible with generous
transparent margin, no crop. Art direction: minimalist Japanese industrial-design
campaign, calm international technology brand, gallery-quality product
photography, sophisticated not flashy. No other text, no brand logo, no second
object, no arrows, no UI, no gradients in a background, no neon, no decorative
circuitry, no colored glow cloud, no people, no watermark. Output one standalone
transparent high-resolution keycap asset.
